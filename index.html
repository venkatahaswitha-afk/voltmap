Data Story

The duck curve is real. Solar floods the grid mid-day, then drops sharply at sunset — exactly when EV demand climbs as commuters return home. This "double peak" is the defining challenge for grid operators in solar-heavy regions like CAISO.

ERCOT's isolation is a hidden risk. At $16/MWh it looks cheap, but Texas cannot import power from neighboring grids during a demand spike — making it uniquely vulnerable to rapid EV load growth.

Best time to charge: 10AM–2PM. Solar is peaking, prices are lowest, and grid stress is minimal. Smart charging incentives that shift demand to this window are the single highest-leverage policy intervention available today.
